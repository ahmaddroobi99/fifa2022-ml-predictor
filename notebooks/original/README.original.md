# FIFA World Cup 2022 match prediction

ML course project: predict the last knockout matches of FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 (17–18 Dec 2022) from historical international results and FIFA 22 player/squad stats.

`Datasets/` holds match and player tables; `Jupter notebooks/` has EDA and modeling notebooks (`Modeling_QFIFA2022.ipynb`, `Final_Prediction.ipynb`, …).

A student predictor built before/during the tournament — not a betting product.

**Stack:** Python, Jupyter, sklearn
